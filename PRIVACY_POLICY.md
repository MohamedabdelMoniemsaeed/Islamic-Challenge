# Privacy Policy for Islamic Challenge (التحدي الإسلامي)

**Last Updated:** September 24, 2026  
**Effective Date:** September 24, 2026  

Welcome to **Islamic Challenge (التحدي الإسلامي)**. Your privacy and trust are paramount to us. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to transparency regarding your personal data and privacy rights.

---

## 1. Summary (Executive Privacy Summary)
* **Zero Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.
* **100% Local On-Device Operation:** All gameplay progress, scores, achievements, and settings remain exclusively on your device within local SQLite/Room storage.
* **No Network Connectivity Required:** The app does not request or require internet access (`android.permission.INTERNET` is not requested).
* **No Third-Party Trackers or Analytics:** There are no tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or third-party analytics integrated into the active game experience.
* **No Account Required:** You can play immediately without registering, creating an account, or providing email/phone credentials.

---

## 2. Information We Store Locally
The application stores game progress data locally on your device via Android's native Room database:
1. **Player Profile:**
   - Experience points (XP)
   - Virtual coins earned through gameplay
   - Current level and player ranking title
   - Total questions answered, correct answers, and incorrect answers
   - Daily gameplay streak and longest streak records
   - Available lifelines count (50/50, Hint, Extra Time, Skip)
   - Daily reward claim records and daily challenge timestamps
2. **Category Progress:**
   - Answer count and accuracy per category (Quran, Seerah, Prophets, Worship, Ramadan, Adhkar, Manners, General Knowledge)
3. **Achievements:**
   - Timestamp and IDs of unlocked in-game badges and milestones
4. **User Preferences:**
   - Sound effects toggle and volume level
   - Music toggle
   - Haptic vibration toggle
   - App theme selection (System / Light / Dark)
   - Interface language preference (Arabic / English)

*All of the above data is stored exclusively in private application sandbox storage on your device and is never transmitted over the internet.*

---

## 3. Permissions Used
The application requests the absolute minimum necessary permissions to function:
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide subtle tactile haptic feedback when selecting answers or unlocking achievements. This permission can be toggled on or off at any time in the app's Settings screen.

**Permissions NOT Requested:**
- No Internet permission (`INTERNET`)
- No Location permissions (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`)
- No Storage permissions (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)
- No Contacts, SMS, Phone, Camera, or Microphone permissions

## 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising
- **Advertising:** None. There are no advertisements or advertising identifiers (AAID) used in this application.
- **Analytics:** None. No user behavior or telemetry is sent to any external server.
- **Cloud Sync:** None. The app operates as an independent offline educational tool.

Islamic Challenge is an educational quiz game suitable for general audiences and families. Because we do not collect, process, or transmit any personal information whatsoever, our app is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not solicit any personally identifiable information from children.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
Since all data is stored exclusively on your device, you have complete control over your data:
1. **In-App Reset:** You can reset all game progress, XP, coins, statistics, and unlocked achievements at any time by navigating to **Settings $\rightarrow$ Reset Game Progress**.
2. **Device Data Clearing:** You can permanently delete all app data by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings (**Settings $\rightarrow$ Apps $\rightarrow$ Islamic Challenge $\rightarrow$ Storage $\rightarrow$ Clear Storage**).
3. **Uninstalling the App:** Uninstalling the application completely deletes all associated local database files.

---

## 7. Security
Your data is protected by Android's application sandboxing architecture, ensuring other applications on your device cannot access your local game database.
